## #05 — Rowing barre, dos fixe (photo corrigée v4)

**Prompt vidéo à copier avec la photo #05 :**

```text
Animate the exact supplied bent-over barbell-row start image. Keep the chrome athlete and camera fixed. The athlete starts hinged forward at 45 degrees ABOVE THE FLOOR. LOCK THE TORSO AND HIP ANGLE FOR EVERY FRAME: hips stay back, pelvis stays still, chest and shoulders never rise, back remains neutral at the same angle, feet and knees remain still. Never stand up, never rock, never perform a deadlift. Two anatomically normal arms and hands hold ONE straight barbell in overhand grip. Pull toward the navel in ONE second, hold ONE second, lower in TWO seconds to straight arms. Tempo 2011. Keep elbows, wrists and hands natural, no twisted arms, no extra limbs, no camera motion, no text.
```

## #09 — Tirage vertical pronation (image à corriger)

**Prompt vidéo pour la future image conforme, pas pour l'essai affiché :**

```text
Animate the correct seated lat-pulldown start photo. Both arms start fully extended overhead, both hands hold ONE long bar in pronation, one taut cable to the top pulley. Feet flat, thighs supported, torso upright and fixed. Pull the bar IN FRONT of the face toward the upper chest in ONE second, elbows down; immediately return overhead in THREE seconds. Tempo 3010, no pauses. Never pull behind the neck, never swing the torso, no extra hands or text, camera fixed.
```

## #10 — Tractions pull-up (nouvelle photo)

La barre de la photo sort du cadre : le prompt demande de conserver une barre fixe, et la fixation devra être vérifiée sur la vidéo. Une répétition complète avec la pause en bas dure **au moins 7 s** ; une génération de 5 s ne peut pas montrer fidèlement ce cycle.

**Prompt vidéo à copier avec la photo #10 :**

```text
Animate the supplied full-extension pull-up start photo. Same chrome athlete and fixed camera, whole body visible. Hang from ONE rigid, fixed overhead bar with both hands in pronated overhand grip. Feet remain OFF the floor; both arms start fully extended and head is below the bar. Hold this bottom position for TWO full seconds. Pull upward in ONE second until the CHIN clears the bar. Without pausing at the top, lower for FOUR full seconds to the original full-arm extension. Tempo 4210, one complete repetition, duration at least SEVEN seconds. No kicking, body swing, floor support, twisted elbows, extra limbs, moving bar, text or camera movement.
```

## #11 — Tractions prise neutre (image à corriger)

La fiche nomme « chin-up » mais précise **prise neutre** : deux poignées parallèles, paumes face à face ; ne pas remplacer par la prise pronation du #10. Alternative : tirage vertical neutre.

**Prompt vidéo pour la future image conforme :**

```text
Animate the correct NEUTRAL-GRIP pull-up start photo. Same chrome athlete, full body visible, fixed camera. The athlete hangs freely from TWO fixed parallel handles, one in each hand, PALMS FACING EACH OTHER throughout. Arms start fully straight, feet off the floor, head below handles. Hold bottom TWO seconds, pull upward in ONE second until chin clears the handles, then immediately lower in FOUR seconds back to straight arms. Tempo 4210; one complete repetition of at least SEVEN seconds. No grip change, no kicking or swinging, no floor contact, no extra arms, no text.
```

## #12 — Rowing haltère un bras, coude ouvert

**Prompt vidéo à copier avec la photo #12 :**

```text
Animate the supplied one-arm dumbbell row start image with the same chrome athlete, flat bench and fixed camera. One hand and knee on the same side stay supported by the bench, opposite foot planted. The OTHER hand alone holds ONE dumbbell, initially hanging below the shoulder and clear of the bench. Keep the back neutral, pelvis and torso stable. Pull the dumbbell outward toward the side of the upper torso in ONE second, working elbow opening to the side in a comfortable range and shoulder blade drawing back. Immediately lower in FOUR seconds to a naturally extended arm. Tempo 4010, no pauses. No torso twist, no exaggerated elbow height, no collision with bench, no second dumbbell or extra limbs, no text.
```

Le dépôt ultérieur d'une vidéo par Yanis vaut accord d'intégration. Contrôler chaque source et l'intégrer avec les réserves réellement observées ; ne pas prétendre valider un mouvement ou un tempo qui n'est pas démontré.
