## #01 — Pompes inclinées, mains surélevées — PRÊT

### Exécution attendue

Mains sur une barre fixe stable, légèrement plus larges que les épaules, pieds
reculés et corps gainé en une ligne tête–hanches–talons. Les coudes restent à
environ 45° du buste.

1. La photo commence en bas, poitrine proche de la barre sans contact forcé.
2. **Pousser en 1 s** en dépliant les coudes, sans casser les hanches.
3. **Descendre en 2 s** vers la barre et terminer exactement à la photo.
   Tempo `2010`, sans pause ajoutée.

### Prompt à copier avec la photo #01

```text
Animate the EXACT same massive silver-chrome athletic man from the supplied image, preserving his matte black cap, black shorts, white sneakers, bright grey gym and stable red-orange pectorals. CAMERA: fixed lateral side view, head LEFT and feet RIGHT, with the full body and fixed support bar visible. START at the exact CONTROLLED BOTTOM position in the image: BOTH HANDS ON THE ELEVATED FIXED BAR slightly wider than shoulder width, chest close to the bar with a small safe gap, elbows bent about 45 degrees back from the torso, wrists straight, and the entire body forming ONE rigid straight line from head through hips to heels. PHASE 1 (1 second): push through both hands and extend the elbows to raise the whole aligned body away from the bar, without bending at the hips. PHASE 2 (2 seconds): bend the elbows and lower the whole aligned body slowly until the chest returns to the supplied bottom position. Show ONE continuous 1-second-up / 2-second-down repetition, tempo 2010, with no added pause. Keep the feet, core, glutes, head-to-heel alignment, support bar and camera fixed; the hands never leave the bar. No floor push-up, no hip sag, no hip pike, no knee push-up, no dumbbells, no extra limbs, no camera movement, no text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## #02 — Développé couché avec deux haltères — PRÊT

### Exécution attendue

Sur banc plat, tête, omoplates et hanches appuyées, pieds au sol. Deux haltères
indépendants en pronation, paumes vers les pieds, poignets empilés et coudes à
environ 45° du buste.

1. La photo commence en bas, haltères à côté de la poitrine sans contact.
2. **Pousser en 1 s** jusqu'au-dessus de la poitrine sans entrechoquer les
   haltères.
3. **Contracter 1 s en haut**, puis **descendre en 2 s** jusqu'à la photo.
   Tempo `2011`, sans pause en bas.

### Prompt à copier avec la photo #02

```text
Animate the EXACT same massive silver-chrome athletic man from the supplied image, preserving his matte black cap, black shorts, white sneakers, bright grey gym and stable red-orange pectorals. CAMERA AND SETUP LOCKED: near-lateral side three-quarter view, head LEFT and feet RIGHT, athlete lying face-up on a completely FLAT HORIZONTAL BENCH with head, shoulder blades and hips supported and feet planted. EXACTLY TWO independent matching dumbbells, ONE in EACH hand, pronated grips with palms toward the feet and wrists stacked. START at the exact BOTTOM position in the image: elbows bent about 90 degrees and tucked about 45 degrees from the torso, forearms vertical, dumbbells just outside the sides of the chest with a safe gap. PHASE 1 (1 second): press both dumbbells upward by extending the elbows until the arms are nearly straight above the chest, keeping the weights separate. PHASE 2 (1 second): hold a controlled chest contraction at the top without moving the torso or banging the dumbbells together. PHASE 3 (2 seconds): lower both dumbbells slowly along the same paths back to the supplied bottom image. Show ONE continuous 1-second-up / 1-second-top-contraction / 2-second-down repetition, tempo 2011, with no bottom pause. Keep the flat bench, head, hips, feet, grip, both dumbbells and camera fixed. No barbell, no incline bench, no neutral grip, no dumbbells touching the face or chest, no merged weights, no extra limbs, no camera movement, no text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## #03 — Développé incliné avec deux haltères — PRÊT

### Exécution attendue

Sur le banc de la photo réglé à 30°, tête, dos, omoplates et hanches appuyés,
pieds au sol. Deux haltères indépendants en pronation, paumes vers les pieds et
poignets alignés.

1. La photo commence en haut, bras presque tendus et haltères au-dessus de la
   poitrine, avec la tête visible et posée.
2. **Contracter 1 s en haut**, puis **descendre en 2 s** en pliant les coudes
   jusqu'à placer les haltères à côté du haut de la poitrine.
3. **Pousser en 1 s** pour revenir exactement à la photo. Tempo `2011`, sans
   pause en bas et sans transformer le mouvement en développé épaules.

### Prompt à copier avec la photo #03

```text
Animate the EXACT same massive silver-chrome athletic man from the supplied image, preserving his matte black cap, black shorts, white sneakers, bright grey gym and stable red-orange pectorals. CAMERA AND SETUP LOCKED: side three-quarter view from the foot end, head LEFT and feet RIGHT, athlete reclined on a 30-DEGREE INCLINE BENCH with the BACK OF THE HEAD, shoulders, back and hips visibly supported and feet planted. EXACTLY TWO independent matching dumbbells, ONE in EACH hand, pronated grips with palms toward the feet and straight stacked wrists. START at the exact TOP position in the image: arms nearly extended and both dumbbells above the upper-chest / shoulder line, with the head and cap fully visible and unobstructed. PHASE 1 (1 second): maintain a controlled chest contraction at the top. PHASE 2 (2 seconds): bend the elbows and lower both dumbbells slowly BESIDE THE UPPER CHEST, keeping the forearms vertical and the elbows about 45 degrees from the torso, with no contact and no pause at the bottom. PHASE 3 (1 second): press the same two dumbbells upward along the same paths back to the supplied top image. Show ONE continuous 1-second-top-contraction / 2-second-down / 1-second-up repetition, tempo 2011. Keep the 30-degree backrest, supported head, reclined torso, feet, grips, separate dumbbells and camera fixed. No barbell, no flat bench, no upright shoulder-press posture, no neutral grip, no dumbbells beside the ears, no merged weights, no extra limbs, no camera movement, no text, no logo, no watermark.
```

### Photo encore nécessaire

Produire d'abord une **position haute** avec le même athlète : banc incliné,
tête appuyée et visible, bras presque tendus, **une barre droite au-dessus du
sternum**, avec un large espace entre la barre et le cou/visage. Les anciennes
positions basses avec barre au cou sont écartées.

### Exécution attendue

Mains en pronation légèrement plus larges que les épaules, pouces enroulés,
poignets empilés, omoplates serrées et hanches appuyées. Depuis la future photo
haute, **descendre en 3 s** vers le haut/milieu du sternum, sans descente vers
le cou, puis **pousser en 1 s** pour revenir à la photo. Tempo `3010`.

### Prompt à utiliser uniquement après obtention de la bonne photo #04

```text
Animate the EXACT same massive silver-chrome athletic man from the supplied TOP image, preserving his matte black cap, black shorts, white sneakers, bright grey gym and stable red-orange pectorals. Keep the exact incline bench, rack and camera orientation from the image, with the head, shoulders and hips supported and feet planted. The athlete holds ONE rigid STRAIGHT BARBELL in TWO pronated hands slightly wider than shoulder width, thumbs wrapped and wrists stacked. START with the arms nearly extended and the bar directly above the MIDDLE OF THE STERNUM / CHEST, with the entire head, throat and face visible and a large clear space between them and the bar. PHASE 1 (3 seconds): bend the elbows and lower the SAME horizontal bar slowly toward the UPPER / MIDDLE STERNUM at the lower edge of the pectorals, keeping the forearms nearly vertical and the elbows moderately tucked; stop with a small safe gap and no pause. PHASE 2 (1 second): press the bar upward along the same chest-line path back to the supplied top image. Show ONE continuous 3-second-down / 1-second-up repetition, tempo 3010. Keep the bar, both grips, bench angle, supported head, hips, feet and camera fixed. No dumbbells, no flat bench, no upright torso, no bar at the neck, no bar in front of the chin or face, no lower-abdomen path, no extra limbs, no camera movement, no text, no logo, no watermark.
```

## Références techniques consultées

- [NASM — Incline Push-Up](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/incline-push-up) :
  support surélevé, ligne du corps et coudes à environ 45°.
- [Gravitus — Dumbbell Bench Press](https://gravitus.com/guides/exercises/dumbbell-bench-press/) :
  deux haltères à côté de la poitrine, coudes à 45–60° et avant-bras verticaux.
- [Verywell Fit — Incline Dumbbell Press](https://www.verywellfit.com/how-to-do-incline-dumbbell-press-4588212) :
  banc à 30–45°, dos appuyé et coudes contrôlés.
- [NASM — Incline Barbell Bench Press](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/incline-barbell-bench-press) :
  prise légèrement plus large que les épaules et installation sur banc incliné.
