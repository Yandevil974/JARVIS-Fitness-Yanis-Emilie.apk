## #22 — Rowing assis câble unilatéral

Assis, pieds sur les appuis et genoux souples, dos neutre. Une main tient une seule poignée à la poulie basse, l'autre reste libre sur la cuisse. Tirez le coude vers la taille sans faire pivoter le buste ; un côté à la fois. La photo ne garantit pas la continuité du câble à chaque image.

```text
Animate ONE seated SINGLE-ARM CABLE ROW from the supplied starting photo. Keep the same chrome athlete and fixed full-body camera. The athlete sits upright with a neutral spine, both feet braced on the footplates and knees softly bent. His RIGHT hand holds ONE D-handle with a secure grip, connected by ONE continuous taut cable to the LOW FRONT PULLEY. His LEFT hand stays empty on his left thigh.

Start with the RIGHT arm extended. In ONE second pull the handle toward the right waist, right elbow traveling backward close to the ribs. Hold ONE second at the waist, then return in THREE seconds until the working arm extends. Tempo 3011. Keep torso and hips stable, no rotation, no swinging, no second handle, no disconnected cable, no extra limbs, no text and no camera movement.
```

## #24 — Pullover haltère plat : historique et nouvelle référence

Le GIF montre un haltère tenu à deux mains et un banc plat. Les essais de photo ne rendent pas une prise des deux mains suffisamment lisible et sûre : ils ont été écartés. Une [nouvelle candidate](./43-DOS-24-PULLOVER-PRODUCTION.md) montre une prise horizontale à deux mains. Pour la vidéo, conserver la tête et le dos appuyés, garder les coudes légèrement fléchis, descendre dans une amplitude confortable et maintenir l'haltère sécurisé à deux mains.

**Ancien prompt (prise verticale), conservé pour historique :**

```text
Animate ONE flat-bench dumbbell pullover from a CORRECT reference image showing a secure TWO-HAND grip on ONE dumbbell. Keep the exact athlete and fixed side/three-quarter camera. Head, upper back and hips remain supported lengthwise on the flat bench, feet planted. BOTH hands securely support the same dumbbell throughout; thumbs are wrapped and the weight cannot slip. Start with the dumbbell above the upper chest, elbows slightly bent.

Lower the dumbbell in a controlled arc behind the head in THREE seconds, moving at the shoulders while keeping the same modest elbow bend. Stop within comfortable shoulder mobility, without excessive back arching. Return above the chest in ONE second, without a pause: tempo 3010. Do not turn the motion into a triceps extension or bench press, do not add a second dumbbell, lose the grip, add limbs, text or move the camera.
```

## #25 — Rowing à l'élastique

Assis au sol, genoux souples, buste neutre ; deux poignées tenues à deux mains. **Vérifier l'élastique et fixer solidement l'ancrage avant de tirer** ; ne pas enrouler l'élastique autour des poignets et ne pas relâcher les poignées sous tension. Tirez vers les côtes sans basculer ; revenez lentement sans laisser l'élastique claquer.

```text
Animate ONE seated RESISTANCE BAND ROW from the supplied starting photo. The athlete sits on the floor, knees comfortably bent, feet planted and torso upright with a neutral spine. Both hands hold their own handles securely, thumbs wrapped. The intact resistance bands remain attached to ONE SECURE fixed low anchor in front for every frame. Begin with both arms extended and light band tension.

Pull BOTH handles toward the lower ribs in ONE second, elbows traveling back near the body, shoulder blades moving naturally. Immediately return the handles in THREE seconds to extended arms, keeping control and light tension. Tempo 3010, no pause. Keep the hips and torso stable, no rocking, no snapping band, no release of handles, no band wrapped around wrists, no extra limbs, text or camera movement.
```
