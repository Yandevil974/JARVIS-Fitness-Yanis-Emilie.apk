## #05 — Élévations latérales haltères, myo-reps

Deux haltères légers, buste stable, bras légèrement souples : monter sur les côtés à hauteur d'épaules au maximum, puis redescendre sous contrôle. Le court clip illustre une répétition de base : il ne peut pas prouver les séries myo-reps.

```text
Animate ONE standing DUMBBELL LATERAL RAISE from the supplied start image. Keep the same chrome athlete, black cap, full body and fixed front camera. Feet planted, torso upright, both wrists comfortable. Exactly two separate light dumbbells, one held firmly in each hand. Raise BOTH arms out to the sides in ONE second, elbows softly bent, no higher than shoulder level. Hold ONE second, then lower both weights in TWO seconds without pause at the bottom. Tempo 2011. No shrug, no body swing, no forced wrist rotation, no curl or shoulder press, no extra limbs, text or camera movement. This is the basic movement, not a partial repetition.
```

## #06 — Développé militaire haltères assis, test 3–5 reps

Deux haltères, dos soutenu et pieds au sol. Une répétition contrôlée, sans cambrer ni forcer l'amplitude. La photo est volontairement éloignée pour réserver de l'espace au-dessus de la tête ; elle ne démontre ni une charge particulière ni une performance maximale.

```text
Animate ONE seated DUMBBELL SHOULDER PRESS from the supplied wide start image. Keep the exact SAME WIDE FRAMING and a fixed front camera: the full body AND BOTH DUMBBELLS must remain entirely visible even when the arms extend overhead. NO zoom in. Athlete sits with back supported and both feet planted, holding one separate dumbbell in each hand, palms forward, wrists aligned above elbows. Press both dumbbells upward together in ONE second, hold ONE second without locking forcefully, then lower smoothly in TWO seconds to a comfortable shoulder-height position. Tempo 2011. No excessive back arch, no weights merging, no extra limbs, text or camera movement.
```

## #07 — Élévations latérales assises

Assis, un haltère dans chaque main, bras libres le long des cuisses, torse stable et pieds au sol. Monter les bras sur les côtés jusqu'à hauteur des épaules au maximum puis descendre sans élan.

```text
Animate ONE SEATED DUMBBELL LATERAL RAISE from the supplied start image. Keep the same chrome athlete, full body and fixed front camera. Athlete remains seated upright on the narrow bench, both feet planted and torso still. BOTH hands firmly hold two separate dumbbells hanging freely outside the thighs, never resting on or passing through the bench. With softly bent elbows, raise both arms out to the sides together in ONE second up to shoulder height at most. Without pausing, lower both dumbbells under control in THREE seconds to the start. Tempo 3010. No shrug, swing, curl, shoulder press, extra limbs, text or camera movement.
```

## #08 — Élévations latérales assises, variante non précisée

Le catalogue ne décrivant pas une variante mécanique distincte et montrant le même GIF que #07, on demande le même mouvement de base sans présumer d'autre angle ou prise. Les répétitions de la fiche restent propres à #08.

```text
Animate ONE SEATED DUMBBELL LATERAL RAISE from the supplied start image. Keep the same chrome athlete, full body, narrow bench and fixed front camera. Athlete stays seated upright, both feet planted. Exactly two separate dumbbells are gripped firmly, one in each hand, and hang freely outside the thighs, never resting on or passing through the bench. Raise BOTH arms out sideways together in ONE second, elbows slightly bent, no higher than shoulder height. Immediately lower both weights in THREE seconds until arms hang at the sides. Tempo 3010. No swinging, no invented grip rotation, no shrug, no curl, no extra limbs, text or camera movement.
```
