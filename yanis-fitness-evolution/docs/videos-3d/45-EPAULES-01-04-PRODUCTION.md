## #01 — Développé militaire haltères assis

Assis, dos soutenu et pieds au sol, deux haltères. Pousser vers le haut sans cambrer, puis redescendre sous contrôle à une hauteur confortable, avant toute douleur. Coudes sous les poignets ; ne pas forcer l'amplitude.

```text
Animate the supplied START image as one SEATED DUMBBELL SHOULDER PRESS. Keep the SAME chrome athlete, cap, upright backrest, fixed front camera and full body. Both feet stay flat, back supported and torso braced. Both hands securely grip TWO separate dumbbells, one in each hand, palms facing forward, wrists stacked above elbows. From shoulder height, press BOTH dumbbells upward together in ONE second, without touching or merging them. Hold at the top for ONE second without hyperextending the elbows. Lower both weights in TWO seconds to a comfortable shoulder-level start, no bottom pause. Tempo 2011. Keep the movement symmetrical: no excessive back arch, no head contact, no extra limbs, no text or camera movement.
```

## #02 — Élévations latérales haltères

Debout, un haltère léger dans chaque main, coudes légèrement souples. Écarter les bras sans élan jusqu'à hauteur d'épaules au maximum, puis redescendre lentement. La fiche mentionne le petit doigt légèrement plus haut ; ne pas imposer de rotation forcée ni douloureuse, garder les poignets confortables.

```text
Animate the supplied START image as one STANDING DUMBBELL LATERAL RAISE. Keep the SAME chrome athlete, fixed front camera and full body. Both feet remain planted, torso upright and still. Exactly TWO separate light dumbbells, one firmly gripped in each hand, remain separate from the thighs. From arms down beside the thighs, raise BOTH arms out to the sides in ONE second, elbows softly bent, to shoulder height at most. Hold ONE second at shoulder level, then lower both arms smoothly in TWO seconds without a bottom pause. Tempo 2011. Keep wrists neutral and comfortable, no forced wrist rotation or swinging, no bent-elbow shoulder press, no shrug, no extra limbs, no text or camera movement.
```

## #03 — Face pull à l'élastique

Élastique en bon état fixé solidement devant à hauteur du visage ; une poignée dans chaque main, jamais autour des poignets. Tirer vers le visage en écartant les mains et en gardant les coudes confortablement hauts, puis revenir lentement, buste stable.

```text
Animate one STANDING RESISTANCE BAND FACE PULL from the supplied start image. Keep the SAME chrome athlete, fixed camera, feet planted and torso upright. ONE resistance band is securely anchored to the fixed post IN FRONT at about face height. BOTH separate hands hold their OWN black handles firmly; both wrists stay free, no band loops on wrists. The two continuous band strands remain attached to their handles and anchor. From arms extended forward, pull both handles toward either side of the face in ONE second, elbows moving outward at comfortable shoulder height, shoulder blades drawing together. Hold ONE second; return forward under control in TWO seconds, no bottom pause. Tempo 2011. No leaning back, no downward row, no snapping band, extra limbs, text or camera movement.
```

## #04 — Face pull à la poulie

Face à une poulie haute, une extrémité de corde dans chaque main, tirer vers le visage en écartant les mains, coudes confortablement hauts. Revenir lentement sans décoller les pieds ni basculer le tronc.

```text
Animate one STANDING CABLE FACE PULL from the supplied start image. Keep the SAME chrome athlete, fixed full-body camera, both feet planted and torso upright. ONE continuous cable runs from the high pulley IN FRONT to the middle of ONE rope attachment. EACH hand grips one separate rope end with a secure thumb-wrapped grip. Start with arms extended forward. In ONE second pull the rope toward the face, separating the hands to either side of the face as the elbows travel outward at comfortable shoulder height. Hold ONE second, then return the rope forward in TWO seconds, without a bottom pause. Tempo 2011. Keep the cable attached and taut. No torso swing, no pulling to waist, no band, no extra limbs, text or camera movement.
```
