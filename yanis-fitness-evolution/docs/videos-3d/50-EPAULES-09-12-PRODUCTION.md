## #09 — Élévations latérales coude à 90°

Debout, deux haltères légers, coudes près du buste et fléchis à environ 90°. Lever les coudes vers les côtés sans changer fortement leur angle, jusqu'à hauteur confortable d'épaules au maximum. Les avant-bras restent vers l'avant ; ce n'est ni un curl ni un développé.

```text
Animate ONE standing BENT-ELBOW DUMBBELL LATERAL RAISE from the supplied start image. Keep the same chrome athlete, fixed camera, feet planted and torso upright. BOTH hands hold separate light dumbbells. Start with upper arms beside the torso and both elbows bent about 90 degrees, forearms pointing forward. In ONE second, lift both ELBOWS OUT to the sides, no higher than shoulder level, while maintaining the same approximately 90-degree elbow bend and forearms pointing forward. Immediately lower both elbows in THREE seconds to the starting position. Tempo 3010. Move at the shoulders, not as a curl or overhead press. No swinging, no changing to straight arms, no extra limbs, no text or camera movement.
```

## #12 — Élévations latérales face au banc incliné 30°

Banc à environ 30° par rapport à l'horizontale, torse soutenu face au dossier, pieds stables, un haltère léger dans chaque main. Monter les bras latéralement depuis les épaules dans une amplitude confortable, coudes légèrement souples, puis redescendre sans décoller le torse. La série dégressive 10/8/6 est une consigne de programmation : une vidéo de quelques secondes ne démontre pas le drop set.

```text
Animate ONE CHEST-SUPPORTED INCLINE DUMBBELL LATERAL RAISE from the supplied start image. Keep the same chrome athlete and a fixed full-body camera. The bench back pad stays inclined at approximately 30 DEGREES ABOVE HORIZONTAL, not 45 or 90 degrees. Athlete lies FACE DOWN with chest supported on the pad and both feet stable. Each hand firmly holds ONE separate light dumbbell; both arms start hanging down freely at the sides of the bench. Raise both arms OUT TO THE SIDES in ONE second, elbows slightly soft, only to a comfortable shoulder-level range. Immediately lower the weights in THREE seconds to the start, tempo 3010. Keep chest on the pad, back neutral and both weights separate from each other, bench and floor. No row, no torso lift, no extra limbs, no text or camera movement.
```
