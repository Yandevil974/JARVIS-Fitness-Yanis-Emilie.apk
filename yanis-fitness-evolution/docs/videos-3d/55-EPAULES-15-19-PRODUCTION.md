```text
Animate ONE SEATED BEHIND-THE-NECK BARBELL PRESS from the supplied image. Keep the SAME chrome athlete, seated upright, both feet planted, and fixed wide rear three-quarter camera. Both hands grip ONE straight horizontal bar, clearly BEHIND and above the head, at a comfortable width. From the supplied position, press the bar vertically upward in ONE second, keeping the entire bar, plates and hands visible. Without pausing, lower it BEHIND the head in FOUR seconds only back to the comfortable starting height. Tempo 4010. The bar must NEVER touch the head or neck; no forced deep descent, no twisting or excessive arching, no movement in front of the face, no extra limbs, text or camera movement.
```
